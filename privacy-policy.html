Section 1

Introduction & Our Ethical Commitment

At AeroPure Solutions, Inc. ("AeroPure", "we", "our", or "us"), we operate at the sensitive intersection of human health, residential and commercial real estate diagnostics, and certified microbiological laboratory science. We understand that indoor air quality data, toxic mold infestation telemetry, and property defect reports are confidential assets of immense personal and financial consequence.

This Privacy Policy explains how AeroPure collects, processes, encrypts, and protects your information across our website, mobile field diagnostic apps, customer portal, user accounts, and AIHA-LAP laboratory chain-of-custody integrations. By engaging our inspection services or utilizing our portals, you acknowledge the practices outlined in this policy.

Section 2

Categories of Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to perform rigorous environmental diagnostics, deliver certified laboratory reports, and coordinate certified remediation protocols:

Client Contact Identifiers

Full name, billing address, inspection site address, direct telephone number, email address, and secondary contact information for co-owners or property managers.

Environmental Telemetry

Raw spore counts per cubic meter (spores/m³), fungal genus classifications, particulate counts (PM2.5 / PM10), relative humidity, thermal imagery, and VOC indices.

Symptom Questionnaires (Optional)

Self-reported respiratory observations, recurring allergies, or historical water leak notices provided to aid field scientists in sample targeting.

Payment Information

Encrypted tokenized billing information. Full credit card numbers are never stored on AeroPure servers; transactions are secured via PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant processors.

Section 3

AIHA Laboratory Chain of Custody & Anonymity

When our field technicians gather Air-O-Cell cassette samples, bio-tape lifts, or bulk cellulose specimens, all samples are immediately coded with random 10-digit alpha-numeric Chain-of-Custody (CoC) Barcodes.

  • Blinded Analysis: Third-party microbiology laboratories receive only sample numbers, flow rates, and room designations (e.g., "Sample #AP-88219 - Master Bedroom"). Laboratories do NOT receive your personal identity or property address.
  • Tamper-Proof Seals: Physical samples are encased in tamper-evident security containers prior to courier shipment.
  • Digital Re-association: AeroPure's encrypted cloud portal matches the returning certified lab analysis back to your secure client record only after microbiology sign-off.
Section 4

Real Estate & Insurer Non-Disclosure Guarantee

Client Confidentiality Policy: The contracting client who pays for the inspection owns the diagnostic report in its entirety.

Many property owners fear that requesting an air quality inspection or mold clearance evaluation will automatically flag their property on public registries or insurance CLUE databases. We adhere to a strict Zero Third-Party Reporting Guarantee:

  • We do NOT transmit inspection outcomes to Multiple Listing Services (MLS) or real estate broker aggregators.
  • We do NOT furnish data to property casualty underwriters or mortgage lenders unless you explicitly request an insurance billing liaison.
  • We do NOT publish residential mold clearance records to municipal registries without a court order or subpoena.
Section 5

Portal Encryption, Storage & Infrastructure

All digital telemetry, client portal communication, and downloadable PDF laboratory reports are defended by multiple layers of enterprise security:

256-Bit AES Encryption

All inspection certificates and laboratory reports are encrypted at rest using AES-256 cryptographic standards.

TLS 1.3 In-Transit

All web traffic between your browser and our servers is secured via modern TLS 1.3 cipher suites.

Role-Based Access (RBAC)

Only certified environmental hygienists assigned directly to your project can access your active file.

Air-Gapped Cold Backups

Redundant disaster recovery vaults ensure historical test records remain secure against ransomware.

Section 6

Cookies & IoT Sensor Telemetry

We employ essential browser cookies and local storage tokens strictly to maintain your session state, preserve your theme preference (Dark Mode / Light Mode), and maintain RTL language preferences.

For commercial facilities deploying our continuous IoT indoor air quality telemetry hardware, sensors stream real-time particulate indices via secure MQTT over TLS. IoT telemetry streams contain zero video or audio recording sensors; only physical air quality metrics (PM2.5, TVOC, CO2, temperature, and relative humidity) are measured.

Section 7

Your Data Rights & Records Retention

Under international data protection frameworks (including CCPA, GDPR, and US state privacy statutes), you maintain the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You may at any time request an export of your full account record and raw laboratory analytical sheets.
  • Right to Rectification: If any property detail or billing note is erroneous, you may request prompt correction.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request the deletion of your account and personal identifiers from our commercial databases.
Statutory Environmental Retention Notice: As certified IICRC environmental contractors, state environmental compliance laws require that signed Chain-of-Custody laboratory certificates be maintained in encrypted, anonymized legal archives for a minimum of seven (7) years following completion of remediation.
