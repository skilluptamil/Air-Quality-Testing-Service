In-Depth Forensic Inspection

Why Professional Laboratory Testing Matters

Visual inspection alone detects less than 20% of residential mold infestations. Microscopic mold colonies routinely establish themselves inside AC evaporator coil plenums, behind insulated drywall, and beneath subfloors without ever showing obvious surface discoloration.

Our certified inspectors employ calibrated high-flow vacuum sampling pumps pulling 75 liters of air through sterile Air-O-Cell cassettes. These samples are compared against an outdoor ambient control sample to definitively prove whether your indoor air is amplifying toxic mold spores.

Toxigenic Threats

Common Mold Strains We Detect & Neutralize

Not all mold is the same. Understanding the exact organism is essential for proper medical diagnosis and targeted containment.

Biohazard Level: High

Stachybotrys chartarum

Commonly known as "Toxic Black Mold"

Grows on cellulose-rich materials (drywall paper, wallpaper, insulation backing) subjected to continuous moisture. Produces potent satratoxin mycotoxins capable of causing chronic hemorrhage, severe fatigue, and neurological impairment.

Biohazard Level: Moderate

Aspergillus / Penicillium

Common HVAC & Dust Pathogen

Easily aerosolized spores that travel rapidly through air ducts. Triggers intense allergic bronchopulmonary reactions, asthma flare-ups, ear infections, and skin irritation in immunocompromised individuals.

Biohazard Level: Allergenic

Cladosporium

Window Sill & Bathroom Colonizer

Thrives on condensation around window frames, tile grout, and subflooring in high-humidity climates. Often leads to chronic sneezing, sinusitis, and persistent throat tickles.
