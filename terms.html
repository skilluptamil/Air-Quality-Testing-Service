Clause 1

Scope of Environmental Diagnostics

AeroPure Solutions provides non-destructive indoor environmental assessments, spore trap cassette air sampling, surface bio-tape sampling, thermal infrared thermography, and relative humidity profiling.

Our inspections represent visual, instrumental, and microscopic sampling of accessible spaces at the precise date and time of inspection. Inspections do not include intrusive or destructive dismantling of finished drywall, subfloors, ductwork interiors, or siding unless a formal destructive invasive investigation has been separately contracted in writing.

Clause 2

Property Access, Structural Attics & Crawlspaces

The client or an authorized representative (such as a real estate agent or property manager) must ensure unobstructed and safe access to all target areas, including utility rooms, basements, HVAC units, and attic crawlway entries.

Technician Safety Standards:

AeroPure field environmentalists retain the professional discretion to refuse entry into spaces exhibiting active bio-hazards (such as raw sewage pooling >2 inches, live uninsulated electrical wiring, aggressive animals, or structurally compromised ceiling joists) until safe conditions are established.

Clause 3

Closed-Building Testing Conditions & Calibration

In accordance with EPA and AIHA protocol guidelines, accurate airborne fungal enumeration requires Closed-Building Conditions for a minimum of 12 (preferably 24) hours prior to air sampling:

  • All exterior windows, sliding patio doors, and exterior dampers must remain closed except for normal entry and exit.
  • HVAC systems may be operated on normal automated thermostat mode; whole-house attic ventilation fans must be turned OFF.
  • High-volume air filtration purifiers (HEPA machines) in target rooms must be turned OFF 24 hours prior to establish baseline resting ambient levels.

Failure to adhere to closed-building conditions may introduce outdoor particulate noise, which AeroPure's laboratory scientists will note on the formal certificate.

Clause 4

100% Post-Remediation Clearance Guarantee

AeroPure is proud to stand behind its certified remediation protocols with our signature Clearance Guarantee under the ANSI/IICRC S520 Standard for Professional Mold Remediation:

Passing Criteria

Total indoor spore counts are at or below outdoor reference levels, with zero elevated indicator species (Stachybotrys, Chaetomium, Memnoniella).

Zero-Cost Re-Treatment

If a containment zone treated by our certified technicians fails PRV laboratory testing, we perform secondary HEPA vacuuming, antimicrobial fogging, and re-testing at our sole expense.

Note: The guarantee is conditioned upon the prompt resolution of any active external plumbing or roof leaks identified in the diagnostic scope of work.
Clause 5

Transparent Fixed Pricing, Lab Fees & Invoicing

All published package rates include on-site diagnostic labor, thermal imaging, calibrated spore trap pump equipment, courier delivery to AIHA accredited laboratories, and digital microbiology certificates.

  • Zero Hidden Lab Surcharges: The fee agreed upon at booking covers the included lab samples. Extra samples requested on-site by the client are billed at our flat, transparent $95/sample rate.
  • Insurance Claims Billing: For insurance claims, line-item estimates are rendered via standard Xactimate® pricing matrices to ensure rapid adjuster approval.
  • Payment Terms: Inspection invoices are payable upon completion of the on-site diagnostic or prior to final release of lab certificates.
Clause 6

Cancellation, Rescheduling & Dispatch Mobilization

Appointments may be rescheduled or cancelled with at least 24 hours advance notice with zero financial penalty.

For cancellations received less than 4 hours before the arrival window or where on-site access is denied upon inspector arrival, a standard trip dispatch charge of $75 applies to offset technician travel and route staging. For emergency 24/7 HAZMAT containment dispatches, mobilization commences immediately upon verbal telephone confirmation.
