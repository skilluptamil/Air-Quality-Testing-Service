Transparent & Methodical

Our 4-Step Scientific Process

From initial thermal scan to final clearance verification, here is how we restore your indoor sanctuary.

1
Thermal & Moisture Scan

We inspect all suspect zones with FLIR infrared cameras and radio-frequency hygrometers to pinpoint hidden moisture intruding into drywall or insulation.

2
Air & Surface Sampling

Calibrated air pumps capture spore volumes inside vs. outdoor baseline air. Tape lifts and swabs analyze active fungal species like Stachybotrys.

3
Containment & Scrubbing

We construct 6-mil poly containment barriers, deploy commercial HEPA air scrubbers to capture 99.97% of particulates, and apply safe botanical biocides.

4
Post-Clearance Guarantee

Independent testing verifies that spore counts are well within safe, healthy guidelines before providing an official Certificate of Clearance.
