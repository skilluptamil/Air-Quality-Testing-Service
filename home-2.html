Early Warning System

Are You Experiencing Any of These Symptoms?

Mold mycotoxins and airborne allergens build up silently in air ducts and carpets. Identify the warning indicators before health suffers.

Chronic Allergies & Asthma

Unexplained nasal congestion, itchy eyes, wheezing, and nighttime breathing difficulty that improves when leaving the building.

Musty or Earthy Odors

Persistent damp, stale smells emanating from AC vents, baseboards, closets, or basements indicating active microbial growth.

Brain Fog & Fatigue

High concentrations of airborne volatile chemicals (VOCs) and mycotoxins can cause lethargy, headaches, and dizziness.

High Humidity >55%

Relative humidity over 55% creates the ideal breeding habitat for Aspergillus and Penicillium mold spores in just 24-48 hours.

Precision Methodology

The Scientific 4-Stage IAQ Testing Protocol

How we measure, culture, and solve indoor contamination.

1
Outdoor Air Baseline

We capture an outdoor control sample to benchmark local atmospheric pollen and natural spore levels for accurate scientific comparison.

2
Multi-Room Indoor Trap

High-flow vacuum pumps pull air in living rooms, bedrooms, and HVAC zones to isolate exact contamination hotspots.

3
Microbiology Lab Analysis

Samples are sent under chain-of-custody to an AIHA-LAP laboratory for cellular microscope enumeration and mold strain typing.

4
Digital Action Plan

You receive a clear, color-coded health dashboard with exact spore counts, health impact analysis, and remediation steps.
