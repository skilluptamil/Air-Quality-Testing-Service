Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about mold testing protocols, insurance coverage, and timelines.

An on-site visual and thermal moisture inspection takes between 60 to 90 minutes for an average home. Air cassette samples are overnighted under strict chain of custody to our AIHA-LAP accredited laboratory partner. Standard lab turnaround is 24 to 48 hours; rush 12-hour turnaround is also available upon request.

In many instances, yes! When mold is the direct result of a sudden and accidental covered peril—such as a ruptured supply line, water heater burst, or storm water intrusion—most major insurance policies provide mold riders. AeroPure writes standardized Xactimate billing estimates and assists you directly through the claim verification process.

For localized remediation (such as a single bathroom or under-sink cabinet), our 6-mil negative air containment barriers isolate the work area completely, allowing you to reside comfortably in unaffected rooms. For widespread HVAC or attic remediation, temporary relocation for 24-48 hours may be recommended for comfort.

A Clearance Certificate (PRV) is an official legal document signed by an independent certified indoor environmentalist confirming that the structure has been sanitized and air spore counts are lower than outdoor reference air. This document is critical when selling real estate or fulfilling tenant lease disclosures.
