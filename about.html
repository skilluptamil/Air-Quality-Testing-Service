Guiding Principles

Our Environmental Health Values

What guides every inspection, air sample, and containment barrier we build.

Radical Transparency

You receive exact raw laboratory chain-of-custody data, spore photographs, and itemized moisture readings without inflated scopes of work.

Eco-Botanical Safety

We never use toxic harsh chemical disinfectants that off-gas secondary VOCs. Our antimicrobial agents are EPA registered and botanical.

Total Containment

We protect unaffected areas using continuous negative air pressure scrubbers, sealing off the remediation zone with airtight zippered barriers.
