Preventive Guidelines

IAQ & HVAC Facility Maintenance Checklist

Routine preventive property maintenance is the single most effective defense against toxic mold amplification, high humidity, and airborne dust mite allergens.

Explore Preventive Coating Services
Filter Replacements

Replace HVAC filters with MERV 11 to 13 pleated media every 60-90 days (or 30 days in homes with shedding pets or recent remodeling).

Relative Humidity Control

Maintain indoor relative humidity between 30% and 50%. Relative humidity exceeding 60% triggers rapid fungal germination within 48 hours.

UV-C Germicidal Sanitation

Service in-duct UV-C germicidal air lamps every 12 months to neutralize bacterial biofilms on indoor AC evaporator coils.

Sump Pump & Crawlspace

Test sump pump float switches and inspect crawlspace 20-mil vapor barriers twice annually before rainy seasonal cycles.
