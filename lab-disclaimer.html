Disclosure 1

Independent AIHA-LAP & ISO/IEC 17025 Accreditation

All physical air cassettes (Air-O-Cell, Micro5, Allergenco-D), surface bio-tapes, and bulk material specimens collected by AeroPure Solutions are analyzed exclusively by independent, third-party microbiology laboratories accredited under the AIHA Laboratory Accreditation Programs (AIHA-LAP, LLC / EMLAP #102988) and meeting ISO/IEC 17025:2017 competence standards.

AeroPure does NOT own or maintain in-house spore counting microscopes. This complete separation between our field diagnostic technicians and independent analytical microbiologists guarantees 100% scientific objectivity, eliminating any incentive to fabricate or inflate fungal counts.

Disclosure 2

Direct Microscopic Spore Enumeration Methodology

Air samples are evaluated using Direct Optical Microscopy at 600x to 1000x magnification. The total number of spores identified on the collection trace is calculated against the calibrated air volume (typically 75 or 150 liters of air sampled at 15 LPM) to extrapolate estimated spore counts per cubic meter (spores/m³).

Spores Counted (Viable & Non-Viable)

Direct microscopy detects both viable (living) and non-viable (dead) spores. Because dead spores still contain allergenic proteins and mycotoxins, optical counting provides complete exposure assessment.

Genus-Level Classification Groupings

Certain fungal spores possess identical optical morphology. For example, Penicillium and Aspergillus cannot be visually differentiated under direct optical microscopy and are grouped as Penicillium/Aspergillus-like.

Disclosure 3

Outdoor Baseline Variations & Weather Volatility

Fungi are omnipresent in the natural outdoor environment. A home or commercial building is never expected to be a sterile surgical cleanroom. In every certified inspection, AeroPure gathers simultaneous outdoor control baseline samples.

Atmospheric Factors Affecting Spore Counts:
  • Wind Gusts & Lawn Mowing: Recent grass mowing or strong winds can temporarily surge outdoor Cladosporium or Alternaria counts by 10,000+ spores/m³.
  • Recent Rainfall: Heavy rain suppresses airborne spore counts during precipitation, followed by a dramatic spike 24-48 hours later as moisture stimulates fungal fruiting bodies.
  • Seasonal Foliage: Autumn leaf decay naturally causes massive ambient outdoor baselines. Indoor levels are always judged in direct relation to outdoor controls taken on the same day.
Disclosure 4

Strict Non-Medical Diagnosis & Health Disclosure

Important Legal & Health Disclaimer:

AeroPure Environmental Solutions is an environmental diagnostic engineering and remediation company. We are NOT medical doctors, toxicologists, or healthcare providers.

Laboratory certificates detail the presence, concentrations, and genera of airborne or surface particulates within a physical structure. Under no circumstances do laboratory test results constitute clinical medical diagnosis, immunological allergy verification, or personal toxicological causation assessments.

Human susceptibility to mold and biological particulates varies exponentially depending on age, genetic predisposition, preexisting respiratory conditions (such as asthma, COPD, or cystic fibrosis), and immune status. If you or members of your household are experiencing chronic symptoms, immediately consult a board-certified physician or allergist.

Disclosure 5

Absence of Federal Numerical Exposure Thresholds

Neither the United States Environmental Protection Agency (U.S. EPA) nor the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) has established permissible exposure limits (PELs) or numerical health-based thresholds for airborne fungal spore concentrations.

Therefore, professional indoor air quality evaluations rely on comparative ecology: healthy indoor environments should demonstrate total spore counts significantly lower than outdoor air, with fungal genera distributions mirroring outdoor ambient biodiversity. Any indoor dominance of moisture-indicating taxa (e.g. Stachybotrys chartarum, Chaetomium, Trichoderma) is indicative of abnormal indoor fungal amplification regardless of raw counts.

Disclosure 6

Concealed Cavities & Non-Destructive Boundaries

Air cassettes capture particulates suspended in breathable ambient air at the time of sampling. They do not penetrate hermetically sealed thermal envelopes, enclosed wall interstitial cavities, subfloor vapor barriers, or sealed HVAC flex ducts.

A "Normal Fungal Ecology (Condition 1)" air sample result indicates that fungal spores are not actively aerosolizing into the living space; it does not guarantee that hidden dormant mold does not exist behind ceramic shower tiles or beneath glued vinyl floor tiles where no airflow connection exists.

Disclosure 7
